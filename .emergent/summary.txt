**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS (REVISED):**
- **Business Model:** A B2B SaaS where store owners subscribe to a plan, register their products, and receive periodic, automated analysis of customer reviews. Billing is based on the analysis frequency and number of products.
- **Core Feature:** Analyze customer reviews to generate four key insights:
    1.  **Strengths (강점):** What to maintain and emphasize in marketing.
    2.  **Suggestions (건의사항):** Recommendations for product/service improvement.
    3.  **Complaints (불만):** Critical issues to fix or decide on dropping the product.
    4.  **New Product Needs (신제품 욕구):** Latent customer needs that could inspire new product development.
- **Insight Assetization:** Suggestions, Complaints, and New Product Needs are to be aggregated and classified by an international standard product classification system (HS Code). This creates a valuable Improvement Asset DB that can provide market-wide insights and checklists for sellers entering a new product category.
- **Payment System:** Must support international payments (Stripe) and major Korean payment gateways (KakaoPay, NaverPay, Toss, etc.).
- **Onboarding:** Sellers must be able to self-service: sign up, get an API key, register their store/products, and receive setup guidance.
